The Future of AI in Education

Published: June 2, 2025 | By: Lucky

Artificial intelligence is transforming how we learn. From personalised learning paths to automated grading, AI tools are making education more accessible and efficient than ever before.

Platforms like Khan Academy and Coursera are already integrating AI tutors that adapt to each student's pace and learning style. Imagine a teacher that never gets tired, never judges, and always meets you exactly where you are.

But it's not without challenges. Questions about data privacy, algorithmic bias, and the role of human teachers remain important. The key is finding the balance — using AI as a tool to enhance human teaching, not replace it.
