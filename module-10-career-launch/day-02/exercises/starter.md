## Exercise 5: CV Review Checklist

Run through this checklist for your CV:

- [ ] Is it one page? (Max two for 5+ years experience)
- [ ] Does it have your name, email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub, and portfolio URL?
- [ ] Is the professional summary specific (not generic)?
- [ ] Are technical skills listed and categorised?
- [ ] Do project descriptions use action verbs and measurable results?
- [ ] Is the formatting simple (no tables, columns, or graphics for ATS)?
- [ ] Have you spell-checked everything?
- [ ] Does it mirror keywords from a target job description?
- [ ] Would someone understand your skills in 10 seconds of scanning?
