## Exercise 5: CV Review Checklist — Notes

All items should be checked. Common issues to fix:

- **Two pages?** → Cut the oldest or least relevant experience
- **Generic summary?** → Replace with specific tech stack and achievements  
- **Missing GitHub/portfolio links?** → Add them in the contact section
- **Bullet points starting with "Responsible for"?** → Rewrite with action verbs: Built, Designed, Implemented, Deployed, Optimised
- **Fancy formatting?** → Switch to single-column, standard fonts (Arial, Calibri), no tables
