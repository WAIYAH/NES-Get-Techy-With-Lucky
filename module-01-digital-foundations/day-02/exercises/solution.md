### Task 5: Phishing Detector

1. Email from `support@paypa1.com` — 🚩 **PHISHING** — The domain uses a "1" instead of "l" in "paypal". Urgency language is a red flag. Legitimate companies don't threaten immediate action.

2. Email from `noreply@github.com` — ✅ **SAFE** — This is a legitimate GitHub domain and a normal notification. But always verify by hovering over links before clicking.

3. Email from `security@bank-verify-account.com` — 🚩 **PHISHING** — The domain `bank-verify-account.com` is not a real bank domain. Banks never send `.exe` attachments. Never download or run unexpected attachments.

4. Email from your colleague's known email with a Google Doc link you were expecting — ✅ **SAFE** — You know the sender, recognise the email, and were expecting the document. Still good practice to verify the link goes to `docs.google.com`.
